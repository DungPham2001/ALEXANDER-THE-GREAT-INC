About us

A dedicated manager behind every property.

Alexander The Great Inc. acts as the Manager of 1 Fortune Realty LLC, a real estate company. This structure is widely used by investors to organize, lease and operate valuable real estate assets under a clear and professional governance framework.

By separating ownership from day-to-day management, we give property stakeholders a single, accountable point of contact, streamlined decision-making and consistent operational standards.

  • Clear governance and accountability
  • Professional, consistent operations
  • Focused on long-term asset value
